THE EVALUATION SETUP
One question.
The whole piece.
Audio questions range from global musical style and structure to local melody, harmony, and rhythm. The model receives the complete track for each question; timestamps and bar ranges locate the passage when needed.
ABC questions pair notation excerpts with natural-language questions. Reference answers, audio annotations, model responses, and compact judge scores support inspection and evaluation.
